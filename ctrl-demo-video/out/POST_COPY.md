# Submission description

CTRL — One world. Everyone wants the keyboard.

A shared aquarium controlled one paid command at a time. Add a fish, change gravity, open treasure or collapse the world with a black hole. 80% of each turn funds its aquarium reward pool; 20% goes to the developer. This demo shows the keyboard economy, a verified Base Sepolia payout, Whale Aquarium and the published limits and trust assumptions.

Try the build: https://ctrl-aquarium.vercel.app/#room

Test credits and fictional souvenirs only. Outcomes can be empty; returns are capped by the available pool. Server-selected randomness, not VRF.
