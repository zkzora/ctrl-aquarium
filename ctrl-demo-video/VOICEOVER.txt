One world. Everyone wants the keyboard. This is Control: a shared aquarium where each person's strange idea becomes the next player's starting point.

Add a shark. Build a shelter. Reverse gravity. Control is the product: buy a keyboard turn, run one command, and pass the changed world to the next player.

Connect your wallet on Base Sepolia and sign in. Claim 1,500 free test credits. Your balance and daily limits follow your wallet when you reconnect.

Public turns cost 10 credits. Whale turns cost fifty. 80% funds the shared reward pool; 20% goes to the developer. Confirm payment, then execution, with no second credit charge.

Our completed testnet run started with 1,440 credits. Adding a fish and exploding one cost 20. The random reward was 30, leaving 1,450. 16 credits went to the pool; 4 to the developer.

Add a fish or treasure to unlock a reward command. Explode a shared fish or open a chest. 60% of base rolls are empty; rewards are capped by the pool.

A black hole uses one paid turn. It rolls for the quoted fish and treasure, swallows the entire world, and starts a new round. Inhabitants return gradually. Both aquariums receive up to 300 fresh pool credits from finite treasury reserves.

Each wallet can buy 20 turns, 5 reward commands, and one black hole per rolling day. Each discovery consumes a contribution. Wallet limits alone do not stop someone using multiple wallets.

Payments, fee splitting and payouts settle on Base Sepolia. The server chooses random results. Commitments show that paid outcomes were not changed; they do not prove independently fair randomness. The rules are published.

Try Control. Inherit the world. Leave it a little stranger. Test credits and stock-inspired souvenirs have no cash value.
